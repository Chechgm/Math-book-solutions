\begin{exercise}{1}
Find operators $T: \R^2 \to \R^2$ such that $T^2 = I$, the identity operator.
Indicate which of the square roots is the positive square root of $I$.
\end{exercise}
\begin{proof}
$I$ and $-I$ are square roots of $I$, $I$ being the positive one.
\end{proof}
