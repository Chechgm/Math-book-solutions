\begin{exercise}{2}
What does Theorem 8.5-1 imply in the case of a system of $n$ linear algebraic equations in $n$ unknowns?
\end{exercise}
\begin{proof}
A system of $n$ linear algebraic equations in $n$ unknowns can be represented as a matrix $A$ with $n$ rows and $n$ columns.
Thus, Theorem 8.5-1 says that the system $A x - \lambda x = (A - \lambda I) x = y$ has a solution if and only if $y$ is a vector such that for all $f \in X' = \C^n$ satisfying $A^\times f - \lambda f = A^T f - \lambda f = 0$ is such that $f(y) = \brackets{f, y} = 0$, that is, $f$ is orthogonal to $y$.
The inner product in here is the dot product, since in finite dimensions, all inner products are isomorphic to the dot product (3.6-5).
\end{proof}
